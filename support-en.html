Frequently asked questions

I changed phone — where is my game?

If you had uploaded your game, it is waiting for you: install the game on the new device, sign in to the same account (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone), then ⚙ Settings → Online save → Retrieve my game.

If you had not uploaded it, it existed only on the old device and we cannot recover it: the game keeps no copy at the publisher's. Upload your game now on the new device — one tap, and the problem cannot come back.

The game is asking me to choose between two games

That is deliberate. Two devices each wrote on their own side since your last exchange, and we never merge two games: adding two treasuries together would build a game the engine could not have produced. Both are shown side by side with their progress; you keep the one you want, and the one you set aside is not destroyed straight away.

I bought lights and they did not arrive

Purchases are delivered as soon as the store confirms them, which can take a moment. If they are still missing:

  1. check your connection and restart the game;
  2. shop → Restore purchases;
  3. if nothing comes back, write to us and attach the store receipt (Google Play or App Store), which carries the order number.

"Restore purchases" does not give my lights back

That is expected, and not specific to this game. Google and Apple can only re-deliver unconsumed purchases: a pack of lights you have spent is, to the store, a completed order. Restoring therefore returns permanent purchases (the offline-time extension, a running subscription), not resources already spent.

What really protects your purchases is the online save: the game it uploads contains your lights. That is the only reason the feature exists.

How do I get a refund?

Payments do not go through us: they go through Google Play or the App Store, and they are the ones who refund.

  • Google Play — play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory
  • App Store — reportaproblem.apple.com

If their procedure does not succeed, write to us: we cannot refund in their place, but we can support a request.

How do I cancel the subscription?

The Monthly Blessing is cancelled where it was taken out, never inside the game:

  • Android — Google Play → Menu → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions;
  • iPhone / iPad — Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Time already paid for stays active until its end date.

The game is in the wrong language

⚙ Settings, right at the top. The two entries are each written in their own language — "Français" and "English" — so that you can find yours without being able to read the other.

Is there any advertising?

No. None, anywhere, and no ad network is integrated.

Are there paid chests or random draws?

One draw only: the scroll, which calls a randomly drawn angel to the Portal and is paid for in lights. The plain one draws everything; the azure or golden one guarantees the colour of the angel's name, the rest still being drawn. Each shows its odds before you take it — the "?" bubble next to it in the Shop —, for your current Trial. Everything sold for real money states exactly what it delivers: a number of lights, a duration extension, a subscription.

How do I erase my data?

  • The game on the device — ⚙ Settings → "Start over", or uninstall the app.
  • The game uploaded online — through Google Play Games ("Delete game data") or your device's iCloud settings. It is on your account, not with us: you control it directly.

The detail is in the privacy policy.

Does the game work offline?

Entirely. A connection is needed for two things only, both optional: buying, and uploading your game.
