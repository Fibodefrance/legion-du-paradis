Applies to the Legion of Paradise app (version 1.0.0) on Android, iOS and in the browser. Last updated: 2026-09-24.

In one sentence

Legion of Paradise collects no personal data and sends nothing back to its publisher. Your game is a file, it lives on your device, and it leaves only if you ask for it to be saved online yourself — in which case it goes to your Google or Apple account, not to a server of ours.

There is no server of ours. The app contains no analytics, no advertising tracker and no behaviour-measuring tool: no network request is ever addressed to the publisher, under any circumstances.

What the app stores, and where

WhatWhere it is writtenWho can read it
Your progress: legion, buildings, gold, quests, settingsA file on your device (sauvegarde.json); browser local storage for the web versionYou alone
The tokens for your in-app purchasesThe same fileYou alone
A randomly drawn game identifierThe same fileYou alone
Your language and volume settingsThe same fileYou alone

The game identifier does not identify a person or a device. It is a number drawn at random the first time you save online, and its only job is to notice that a second device has written since your last exchange, so the app can ask you which of the two games to keep instead of silently overwriting one. It is derived from no hardware, advertising or account identifier. Erasing the game erases it.

What leaves the device — and only if you ask

Online save

It is optional and never automatic: nothing leaves until you open ⚙ Settings → "Online save" and press "Upload my game".

  • On Android, the game is stored in Google Play Games saved games, on your Google account. Google keeps it for you, and we have no access to it.
  • On iPhone and iPad, it is stored in iCloud, on your Apple account. Same thing: that is your space, not ours.
  • On desktop and in the browser there is no online save at all; the game says so explicitly in that window.

What leaves is the content described in the table above, and nothing else: a saved game. No name, no address, no contacts, no location.

To remove a stored game, use the Google Play Games or iCloud settings on your device; they hold it, and they alone can erase it.

In-app purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. The app never sees your payment method, your name or your billing address: it receives a token from the store saying "this purchase is valid", and that is all.

As a publisher we receive aggregated sales reports from Google and Apple — amounts and countries, never a buyer's identity. How Google and Apple handle your payment data is governed by their own privacy policies, not by this one.

Signing in to Google Play Games (Android)

If you use the online save on Android, Google Play Games asks you to sign in to your Google account. That sign-in happens between you and Google: the app receives what it needs to read and write your saved game, and asks for neither your e-mail address, nor your friends list, nor your profile.

Advertising

This version of the app contains no advertising and integrates no ad network. No advertising identifier is read, no profile is built, and there is therefore no sharing with advertising third parties and no tracking of any kind.

Optional advertising is planned, and it will always be optional. The game features a character, Virgil, from whom you may ask three favours a day; the day an ad network is actually integrated, each of them will be paid for with an advert you chose to watch. There will never be an advert that opens by itself — not between screens, not at launch, not as a banner. Never going to see Virgil means never seeing a single advert, and the game advances just the same.

The day that network goes live, your explicit consent will be asked before the first advert, declining will be possible and will affect nothing else in the game, and it will remain revocable at any time from Virgil's window. This page and the declarations filed with both stores will be updated before that goes live: an ad network reads the device's advertising identifier, and that will have to be written here in plain words.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children and asks them for no information. Since it collects no personal data at all, it collects none from a minor either. It does however contain in-app purchases: the Google Play and App Store parental controls can restrict them.

Your rights

The European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability over your personal data.

We hold no personal data about you: there is therefore nothing for us to send you, nothing to rectify and nothing to erase on our side. The only data that exists is on your device and on your Google or Apple account, where you control it directly:

  • Erase the local game: uninstall the app, or ⚙ Settings → "Start over".
  • Erase the online game: through the Google Play Games settings ("Delete game data") or iCloud settings on your device.

You may nevertheless write to us at any time at legionduparadis@gmail.com; we answer within 5 working days. If you are in the European Union you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Security and retention

As nothing is transmitted to the publisher, there is no database of ours to protect and no retention period for us to set. The local game lives as long as the app is installed; the online game lives as long as your Google or Apple account keeps it.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with its date. A change affecting what the app transmits — adding advertising, for instance — would be announced inside the app before taking effect.
