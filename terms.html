Your game

  • Your game is a file on your device. The online save puts a copy on your iCloud or Google account, so you can find it on another device. Without it, uninstalling the game or changing devices loses the game, along with soul shards bought and not yet spent: "Restore my purchases" does not bring them back.
  • "Start over" erases the whole game, including what was bought; the game asks first and has you confirm.
  • A game file modified outside the game may stop working.

Online play

  • The online Colosseum (battles, duels, Siege, reinforcements, friends, guilds and their chat) is optional. It needs an Internet connection and an online account, created when you first enter, under a randomly drawn identifier.
  • Play fair: modifying the game or what it sends, exploiting a flaw, changing the device's clock or automating the game to climb the rankings is forbidden.
  • Stay courteous: in the chat and in guild names, no insults, harassment, hate, sexual content or personal contact details. Texts are filtered; you can report a message or a guild name, and block a player. Reports are read within 24 hours.
  • In case of a breach, the publisher may hide a message or a guild name, mute its author, and remove a Legion from the rankings and from the Legions offered to other players. Your game on the device is never touched.
  • You can delete your online account at any time: since version 1.0.8, ⚙ Settings → About → "Delete my online account"; otherwise by writing to legionduparadis@gmail.com.
