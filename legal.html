The app

Legion of Paradise, version 1.0.0 — an offline management and progression game, distributed on Google Play and the App Store, and playable in a browser.

The app is free. It offers optional in-app purchases granting access to in-game resources and convenience features. On iPhone and iPad, it contains optional adverts, supplied by Google AdMob: players ask Virgil for them, in exchange for a favour, and none opens by itself. On Android and in the browser, it integrates no ad network.

Every real-money purchase states exactly what it delivers. Only one in-game item involves chance: the scroll, paid for in lights, which calls a randomly drawn angel. Its odds are shown in the game before you take it.
