A management and progression game, in a Paradise of white marble and gold.

You command a legion of angels. They descend the nine circles of Hell, bring back what it takes to build the celestial city, and the city returns a stronger legion. They fight on their own: you decide.

  • Three choirs, four classes: Warrior, Paladin, Archer, Sorcerer.
  • Nine circles, their guardians, and a Well to descend every day.
  • A city to build: forge, trading post, refectory, cloister, bounty office.
  • Seventy-nine quests, and an Ascension once everything is done.
  • In French and English. No advertising.

In short

Legion of Paradise collects no personal data. Your game is a file on your device; it leaves only if you ask for it to be uploaded, and it then goes to your own Google or Apple account.
